## 更新内容（中文）

从 1.0.0 升级：解压新版本即可（可以覆盖旧文件夹）。设置、播放列表和媒体库缓存都保存在 `%LOCALAPPDATA%\GnomeMusicWinUI`，会保留下来；原来的“收藏歌曲”和“最近播放”会自动沿用。

### 播放列表
- 原来的六个智能播放列表（最多播放、从未播放、最近播放、最近添加、收藏歌曲、标记不足）改为两个软件自带的列表：**收藏歌曲**和**最近播放**。它们下面是一条分隔线，再往下是你自己的播放列表（新建的在前）。
- **收藏歌曲**就是加了星标的歌曲，按你自己的顺序排列：新加星标的歌曲排在最前；从列表中移除一首歌会取消它的星标（可以撤消）；可以拖动歌曲调整顺序。
- **最近播放**是最近播放过的 100 首歌，最新的在前。一首歌开始播放时就会排到最前面。这个列表只能看：不能拖动排序，也不能添加或移除歌曲。
- 升级后第一次打开时，这两个列表会沿用已有的数据：收藏歌曲按歌曲加入媒体库的时间排列（最新的在前），最近播放按上次播放的时间排列。
- 播放列表侧栏底部新增“新建播放列表…”：先输入名称，再创建。
- 右键点击侧栏里的播放列表可以播放它；你自己的播放列表还可以“重命名…”和删除（删除可以撤消）。播放列表页里仍然可以直接改名。
- “添加到播放列表…”里也有“收藏歌曲”（添加进去就等于加星标），每个列表前面都有图标。
- 播放次数和上次播放时间不再用来生成播放列表，可以在歌曲属性里查看。
- 修复：在其他页面向播放列表添加或移除歌曲后，回到播放列表页时歌曲数量可能还是旧的（例如一直显示“0 首歌曲”）。

### 歌词
- 歌词页换到下一句时会平滑滚动（400 毫秒，先快后慢）。这个动画由软件自己完成，所以关闭了 Windows 动画效果时（例如通过远程桌面使用）也会有；以前这种情况下会直接跳到下一句。
- 新增**歌词缓存**：下载的歌词可以保存在软件自己的数据文件夹里，不写进音乐文件夹。首选项 → 播放器控制 → 歌词 新增“歌词保存位置”（歌曲文件夹 / 歌词缓存），只有开启“从云端下载歌词”时才能选。
- “加载本地歌词”现在先读歌曲旁边的 `.lrc` 文件，再读歌词缓存；两者都没有时才去 LRCLIB 查找。
- 新增“不重复在云端寻找无结果歌词”（默认关闭）：LRCLIB 上找不到歌词的歌曲会记录在歌词缓存的 `not-found.txt` 里，以后不再去查，直接显示“没获取到”。之后在歌曲属性里为它保存了歌词，它就会自动从记录里去掉；清除歌词缓存会清空这份记录。
- 歌词页左上角新增返回按钮（再次点击歌曲或按 Esc 也可以返回）。播放时指针静止或离开窗口，它会和播放栏、窗口按钮一起隐藏。
- 右键点击歌词页新增“管理歌词”，直接打开这首歌属性里的歌词页。
- 歌曲属性的“歌词”页需要同时开启“歌词”“加载本地歌词”和“从云端下载歌词”；没有全部开启时只显示提示和一个前往“播放器控制”的按钮，页面上的功能（设定为纯音乐、编辑、搜索）都不能用。
- 歌曲属性的“编辑”会打开本地歌词：有歌词文件就打开歌词文件，没有就打开歌词缓存里的歌词。
- 在歌曲属性里下载歌词时，已有本地歌词的会在确认后替换它；没有本地歌词时按“歌词保存位置”保存，按钮会写明“下载到歌曲文件夹”或“下载到歌词缓存”。
- 早期版本以 JSON 格式存在 `lyrics` 文件夹里的歌词不再读取，清除歌词缓存时会一起删掉。

### 小窗
- 小窗可以拖动边缘和四角来缩放，始终保持 1:1 的正方形，最小 200 px；小于 240 px 时隐藏歌名和歌手，只显示按钮。
- 首选项 → 播放器控制 → 小窗 新增“小窗模式”，有三种：
  - **专辑封面**（默认）：和以前一样，指针移到小窗上时，在模糊的封面上显示控制按钮。
  - **播放控制**：控制按钮一直显示在模糊的封面上。
  - **歌词**：显示纯白底的歌词，样式和歌词页一样，颜色跟随专辑封面；字更大、行数更少，保证小窗里也看得清。不能点击歌词跳转，不能手动滚动，没有右键菜单，也不显示进度条；换句时同样平滑滚动。指针移到小窗上时，模糊的是白底歌词本身（和专辑封面无关），并加一层暗色蒙版，让白色按钮和文字看得清楚。纯音乐、找不到歌词的歌曲以及歌词加载中时，会暂时显示专辑封面，设置里有一句提示说明。

### 播放栏、窗口和任务栏
- 任务栏缩略图下新增“上一首 / 播放暂停 / 下一首”按钮，主窗口和小窗都有。按钮一直在，不能用时变灰；图标颜色跟随任务栏的浅色 / 深色主题，缩放比例改变时也会重新绘制。
- 窗口大小不变时，进度条长度保持一致，不再随歌名、歌手的长短或是否开启“显示位深与采样率”而变化：歌曲信息最多占 200 px（窗口较窄时更少），太长的文字以省略号结尾，指针悬停可以看到完整内容。
- 所有页面（包括歌词页）的播放栏上方都有一条清楚的分隔线。
- 还没有音乐时（没有添加音乐文件夹，或文件夹里没有能播放的歌曲），标题栏只显示 ☰ 和窗口控制按钮，首选项里也一样（首选项里另外有返回按钮）；欢迎页不显示返回按钮。媒体库为空时，启动时也不会再闪一下视图切换和搜索按钮。
- 返回按钮在没有地方可以返回时直接隐藏（以前是变灰）。
- 正在播放（或暂停）的歌曲离开媒体库时（它所在的文件夹被移出音乐文件夹或被删除），会停止播放、清空播放队列，播放栏随之消失。
- 修复：没有音乐时用 Tab 键移到 ☰ 会直接打开首选项并盖住欢迎页。现在 ☰ 不再是 Tab 停靠点，键盘用 Ctrl+, 打开首选项。

### 首选项
- 新增“重置”页：
  - 清除歌词缓存：显示缓存里的文件数和大小；歌曲文件夹里的歌词文件不受影响。
  - 清除播放计数：所有歌曲的播放次数归零；收藏、上次播放时间和最近播放都保留。
  - 还原所有设置：所有设置恢复默认值；音乐文件夹和窗口大小保留。
  - 每一项都会先询问确认。
- 设置项下面的说明小字都去掉了，只保留两条提示：
  - 小窗模式选“歌词”时：纯音乐或找不到歌词的歌曲会暂时显示专辑封面。
  - 音量控制下面：“注意：关闭软件音量控制后软件输出音量则固定为100%”。
- DSD 模式只在当前的输出方式不支持所选模式时才显示提示。
- 首选项侧栏的各项现在有无障碍名称，屏幕阅读器可以正确读出。

## What's new (English)

Upgrading from 1.0.0: unzip the new version (over the old folder if you like). Settings, playlists and the library cache are kept in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\GnomeMusicWinUI` and stay; your favorite songs and play history carry over.

### Playlists
- The six smart playlists (Most Played, Never Played, Recently Played, Recently Added, Starred Songs, Insufficiently Tagged) are replaced by two of the app's own: **Favorite Songs** and **Recently Played**. A line follows them, then your own playlists, newest first.
- **Favorite Songs** holds the starred songs in your order: a song you star goes to the top, removing a song from the list unstars it (with Undo), and songs can be dragged into another order.
- **Recently Played** is a history of the last 100 songs, the latest first. A song goes to the top as soon as it starts playing. It is read-only: no reordering, adding or removing.
- The first time after the upgrade, both start from what you had: the starred songs by when they were added to the library (newest first), and the songs by their last play.
- *New Playlist…* at the foot of the playlists sidebar asks for a name, then creates the playlist.
- Right-clicking a playlist in the sidebar plays it; your own playlists can also be renamed (*Rename…*) and deleted (with Undo). The playlist's page still renames inline.
- *Add to Playlist…* also lists Favorite Songs (adding stars the songs), with an icon for each playlist.
- Play counts and last-played dates no longer make playlists; they show in the song's properties.
- Fixed: after adding songs to a playlist or removing them from another page, the playlist's page could show a stale song count (such as "0 songs").

### Lyrics
- The lyrics glide to the next line (400 ms, easing out). The app animates them itself, so this works even with Windows' animation effects off (as over Remote Desktop), where they used to jump.
- New **lyrics cache**: downloaded lyrics can go to the app's own data folder instead of the music folders. Preferences → Player Controls → Lyrics has a new *Save lyrics to* (the song's folder or the lyrics cache), available while *Download lyrics* is on.
- *Load local lyrics* now reads the `.lrc` file next to the song, then the lyrics cache, and asks LRCLIB only when there is neither.
- New *Don't search online again for lyrics not found* (off by default): songs LRCLIB has no lyrics for are listed in the cache (`not-found.txt`) and not looked up again; they show "No lyrics found" at once. Saving lyrics for such a song later (from its properties) takes it off the list; clearing the lyrics cache forgets them all.
- The lyrics page has a back button at the top left (clicking the song again or Esc still works). While a song plays and the pointer rests or is away, it steps aside with the player bar and the caption buttons.
- Right-clicking the lyrics page offers *Manage Lyrics*, which opens the song's properties at their lyrics page.
- The song properties' *Lyrics* page needs *Lyrics*, *Load local lyrics* and *Download lyrics* all on; otherwise it says so, with a button to Player Controls, and nothing on it (instrumental, edit, search) can be used.
- *Edit* in the song's properties opens the local lyrics: the lyrics file, else the cached lyrics.
- Downloading lyrics in the song's properties replaces the local lyrics once confirmed; without local lyrics it saves them where *Save lyrics to* says, and the button says so (*Download to Song Folder* or *Download to Lyrics Cache*).
- The JSON lyrics that earlier versions kept in the `lyrics` folder are no longer read; clearing the lyrics cache deletes them too.

### Mini player
- The mini player resizes from its edges and corners, always as a square, down to 200 px; below 240 px the song's title and artist hide and only the buttons show.
- Preferences → Player Controls → Mini Player has a new *Mini player shows* with three modes:
  - **Cover** (the default): as before; pointing at it shows the controls over the blurred cover.
  - **Controls**: the controls over the blurred cover all the time.
  - **Lyrics**: the lyrics on white, styled like the lyrics page in the cover's color, larger and fewer lines to stay readable in the small window. They cannot be clicked, scrolled or right-clicked, there is no progress bar, and they glide to the next line too. Pointing at it blurs the white lyrics themselves (not the cover) under a dark veil, so the white buttons and text stay clear. Instrumentals, songs without lyrics, and songs whose lyrics are loading show the cover for the time being, as a note in the preferences says.

### Player bar, window and taskbar
- Previous, play/pause and next buttons under the taskbar thumbnail, for the main window and the mini player. They are always there and disabled when they can't be used; their icons follow the taskbar's light or dark theme and are redrawn when the scale changes.
- The progress bar keeps the same length for the same window size, whatever the length of the title and artist, and with or without *Show Bit Depth and Sample Rate*: the song's info takes up to 200 px (less in narrow windows), longer text ends in an ellipsis, and hovering shows it whole.
- A clear line separates the player bar from the page above it, on every page including the lyrics.
- Without music (no music folder, or none with songs the app can play) the title bar shows only ☰ and the caption buttons, in the preferences too (plus the back button there); the welcome page has no back button. An empty library no longer flashes the view switcher and search at start.
- The back button hides when there is nowhere to go back to (it used to be disabled).
- When the song playing (or paused) leaves the library (its folder is removed from the music folders or deleted), playback stops, the queue empties and the player bar goes away.
- Fixed: without music, tabbing to ☰ opened the preferences over the welcome page. ☰ is no longer a tab stop; the keyboard opens the preferences with Ctrl+,.

### Preferences
- A new *Reset* page:
  - *Clear lyrics cache*: shows how many files the cache holds and their size; lyrics files next to the songs stay.
  - *Clear play counts*: every song's play count back to zero; favorites, last-played dates and Recently Played stay.
  - *Reset all settings*: every setting back to its default; the music folders and the window size stay.
  - Each one asks first.
- The descriptions under the settings are gone, except two notes:
  - With the mini player's *Lyrics* mode selected: instrumentals and songs without lyrics show the cover for now.
  - Under *Volume Control*: with it off, the app's output volume is fixed at 100%.
- The DSD mode shows a note only when the current output can't use the chosen mode.
- The preferences sidebar's items have accessible names, so screen readers read them correctly.

### Other

- Everyday CI builds are numbered after the project's version (for example 1.0.1-ci.3).
- Each version's release notes live in the repository's `release-notes` folder and become the release's notes when it is published.
